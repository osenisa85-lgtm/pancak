Pancakes

Description

Fluffy, golden pancakes that come together in about 20 minutes.

Serve them with syrup, fruit, or butter for an easy breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tablespoons melted butter

Steps

  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.
  2. In another bowl, mix the milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and stir until just combined.
  4. Heat a lightly oiled pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter per pancake.
  6. Cook until bubbles form, flip, and cook until golden on both sides.
