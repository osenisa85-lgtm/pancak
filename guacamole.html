Guacamole

Description

A fresh, creamy dip made from ripe avocados, lime, and a few simple seasonings.

Perfect with tortilla chips, tacos, or spread on toast.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe avocados
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped red onion
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 2 tablespoons chopped coriander
  • 1 clove garlic, minced

Steps

  1. Cut the avocados in half, remove the pits, and scoop the flesh into a bowl.
  2. Mash with a fork to your preferred texture.
  3. Stir in the lime juice and salt.
  4. Fold in the onion, tomato, coriander, and garlic.
  5. Taste, adjust the seasoning, and serve right away.
